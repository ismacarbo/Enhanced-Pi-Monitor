AutoIrrigation

A field device platform that treats irrigation as its first domain—not its architectural limit.

Generic protocol, concrete device

AutoIrrigation combines environmental sensors, embedded controllers and non-blocking actuation with a versioned Generic Device Protocol. Protobuf payloads travel over MQTT between ESP32 nodes and a modular Raspberry Pi server, while a local CYD touchscreen provides direct control and feedback.

Stack: ESP32 · C++ · nanopb · MQTT · Mosquitto · Protocol Buffers · Python · Flask · Raspberry Pi

Architecture

Domain messages → Generic Device Protocol → Protobuf → MQTT, with environment, irrigation and power implemented as independent domains.
